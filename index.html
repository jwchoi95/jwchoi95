About the Lab

AI for Digital Transformation & Societal Change

AI-ON Lab investigates how artificial intelligence and machine learning can support digital transformation and help explain its broader organizational, technological, policy, and societal consequences. Our research combines data-driven methods with real-world problems in business, innovation, public policy, and society.

Research Areas

Management of Technology

Technology opportunity discovery, technology valuation, forecasting, and technology intelligence from patent and science data.

Science & Technology Innovation Policy

AI-enabled analysis of science, technology, innovation systems, and policy transformation.

Business Intelligence

Managerial insights from social media, trademarks, online communities, product reviews, and other digital traces.

Applied Machine Learning

Interpretable and task-oriented ML for classification, regression, representation learning, and decision support.

Natural Language Processing

Information extraction, text classification, semantic representation, and knowledge graph construction.

Large Language Models

LLM-based knowledge extraction, cross-domain knowledge alignment, and agentic systems for organizational and policy applications.
